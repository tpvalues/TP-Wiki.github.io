Welcome to the Third Position Wiki

A place for learning, reference and careful archiving

This site exists to collect and present information about the Third Position in a clear, organised and readable way. It is meant to be a practical tool for anyone who wants to study the subject seriously: historical movements, the ideas behind them, the people who shaped them, the texts they left behind, and the smaller details that rarely make it into short summaries.

You will find pages on ideologies, organisations, individual figures, their published works, political activity, internal debates, and the occasional fun fact that helps put things in context. The focus is on Third Position thought and the groups that openly identified with it or developed its core themes.

The wiki also covers a range of associated movements that are historically or ideologically connected, even when they do not sit neatly inside a strict Third Position definition. These are kept in their own section so the distinction remains visible.

A dedicated Definitions page is available to clarify key terms, labels and distinctions used throughout the site. It is a good starting point if you want to understand how categories are applied here.

Everything is still being built. New pages will appear as material is added and checked. The goal is simple: create a calm, useful archive that makes the material easier to find, compare and return to later.

Browse the sections on the left, use the search bar at the top, or start with the Definitions page if you prefer to get oriented first.
