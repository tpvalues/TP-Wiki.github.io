Welcome to the Third Position Wiki. This project documents Third Position thought, related political movements, and the individuals who shaped them. Pages about general nations, mainstream ideologies or non-TP topics redirect to the corresponding article on English Wikipedia.

Featured movement

Union Movement

British political organisation founded by Oswald Mosley in 1948 as successor to the British Union of Fascists. It advanced the idea of "Europe a Nation" and pan-European nationalism in the post-war period.

Founded 1948 · Dissolved 1973 · Ideology: Europe a Nation, Pan-European nationalism

About this project

The Third Position Wiki is an independent reference project focused exclusively on Third Position ideologies, movements and personalities.
